# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Agricultural analysts, risk modelers, insurance underwriters, and carbon credit auditors (RiskGuard, MRV Carbon, Crop Insurance use cases). They need to quickly assess cropland loss and urban encroachment risk for specific zones.

## Product Purpose
Visualise 10m/px native land cover maps across pre-registered zones or arbitrary user-defined bounding boxes (drawn on map or entered manually). Track historical changes (2017–2023), project future growth up to 2041 using a validated U-Net forecasting model, and compute the Agricultural Buffer Index (ABI) to grade and alert users of urban encroachment.

## Brand Personality
Expert, analytical, premium, trustworthy.

## Anti-references
- Saturated SaaS-cream/beige backgrounds.
- Flat layouts with low-contrast gray text.
- Over-rounded cards (border-radius > 16px) and heavy text gradients.
- Generic AI-scaffold eyebrows and numbering (e.g. 01 / 02 / 03).

## Design Principles
- **Clarity Serves Function**: Data visualisations and indexes are primary. Spacing and visual weight should draw eyes to critical metrics (ABI, Crop Loss).
- **Premium Restraint**: Avoid decorative elements like glassmorphism default or sketchy SVGs. Use solid, crisp borders and subtle micro-animations for interactivity.
- **High-Contrast Typography**: Body text and numbers must remain readable. Avoid muted-gray text.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Contrast ratios of at least 4.5:1 for all text.
- Reduced motion support for animations.
